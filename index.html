A practical stance

AI accelerates the work.
Humans keep the product honest.

I use AI to explore ideas, surface edge cases, and move from concept to implementation faster. Product direction, user empathy, system boundaries, and final decisions stay human-owned, so the result comes from shared understanding between people and AI, not blind automation.

How I work

Start with the
real problem.

I use AI tools to move faster, but product direction and understanding remain shared human responsibilities. The goal is software that solves a real need and earns its place in someone’s workflow.

01

Understand before building

Clarify who the product serves, what is getting in their way, and what a useful outcome actually looks like.

02

Turn complexity into a path

Shape the problem into a focused experience, making the next useful step obvious for both the user and the team.

03

Build together with AI

Use AI to explore, question, and accelerate implementation while people own context, judgment, and the final product.
