FLOOSS Wallet

Reframing a cards experience as a wallet for a customer-facing fintech app.

Swift · UIKit · API integrationFintech · onboarding · paymentsApp Store ↗
Overview

FLOOSS is a customer-facing fintech application in Bahrain. I worked on the iOS product team across the cards, wallet, dashboard, and onboarding experiences.

Decisions I owned +

I replaced the cards experience with a production wallet, designing the UI and integrating APIs for real-time balance, payments, and transaction history. I also revamped dashboard quick actions with a compositional layout.

Technical shape +

The work combined Swift/UIKit product delivery with API integration, third-party identity verification, document scanning, selfie capture, refresh eKYC flows, and initial unit-test coverage.

Outcome +

The experience gave customers a clearer way to understand and act on their money, while making room for stronger onboarding and test coverage.
