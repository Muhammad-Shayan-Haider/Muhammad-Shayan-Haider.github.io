Aliv Events

An event-management app spanning discovery, tickets, scanning, checkout, and notifications.

Swift · Combine · MVVMAuthentication · tickets · checkoutApp Store ↗
Overview

Aliv Events brings event discovery and ticket management into one mobile experience, covering signup, home, profile, current tickets, ticket scanning, purchase, checkout, notifications, and FAQs.

Decisions I owned +

I designed the mobile UI flows across the core customer journey and used Combine with MVVM to keep presentation logic testable and the experience responsive.

Technical shape +

The app separated presentation concerns from interaction and state handling through MVVM, with Combine coordinating UI updates across authentication, discovery, ticketing, and checkout flows.

Product surface +

The product covered both sides of attending an event: finding something worth attending and having a reliable path from ticket purchase to entry.
