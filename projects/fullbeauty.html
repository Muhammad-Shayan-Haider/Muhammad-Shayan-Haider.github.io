FullBeauty Brands

A shared UIKit foundation made two distinct e-commerce brands feel native to themselves.

Swift · UIKit · Protocol-oriented designNot live yet · private product work
Overview

The dual-brand e-commerce application served Eloquii and Woman Within from a shared Swift/UIKit codebase. The product needed brand distinction without duplicating the customer journey or making every feature change twice.

Decisions I owned +

I designed and implemented a protocol-oriented BrandTheme architecture for fonts, colors, spacing, components, service keys, and feature flags. I applied it across more than 35 customer-facing screens.

I also reorganized scattered ViewModels and cell implementations into a feature-based structure, connected Interface Builder outlets across 30+ view controllers and cells, and delivered accessibility and UI quality fixes.

Technical shape +

The architecture kept brand-specific configuration at the edge while shared UI and feature behavior stayed reusable. Runtime theme configuration also supported an AI-generated image disclosure footer.

Outcome +

More than 6,000 lines of legacy code were reduced through targeted refactoring, while the shared foundation supported two differentiated storefront experiences.
